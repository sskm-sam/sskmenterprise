# SSKM Website Alignment

## 1. Business Goals

- Make consulting the primary growth engine and generate qualified architecture and SAP opportunities.
- Establish SSKM as a trusted source for professional training, coaching, and practical technology education.
- Build an owned audience through YouTube, travel content, short films, and digital media.
- Convert authority into consultation bookings, proposals, training engagements, course registrations, and media partnerships.

## 2. Target Audience

- Canadian and North American organizations planning SAP transformation or integration modernization.
- CIOs, CTOs, technology leaders, and program sponsors who need senior architecture direction.
- SAP, integration, and enterprise technology professionals seeking training or coaching.
- Audiences interested in SAP tutorials, architecture careers, travel stories, and short films.

## 3. Brand Positioning

Enterprise Technology Consulting, Professional Training, and Digital Media Solutions.

Core personal brand:

- SAP Solution Architect
- Enterprise Architect
- Technical Program Leader
- Trainer & Mentor
- Content Creator

## 4. Site Map

- Home: value proposition, services, credibility, testimonials/proof, and calls to action.
- Services: SAP Consulting, Enterprise Architecture, Integration Architecture, and IT Advisory.
- Training: corporate training, coaching, and workshops.
- Courses: SAP CPI, Integration Suite, Enterprise Architecture, and Solution Architect Bootcamp.
- Media: YouTube, travel vlogs, short films, and production services.
- About: story, credentials, certifications, and leadership.
- Contact: consultation booking, discovery call, proposal request, and contact form.

Implemented routes include `/`, `/training`, `/courses`, `/media`, `/about`, and `/contact`. Existing detailed SAP service routes remain under `/services`.

## 5. Content Strategy

- Lead with consulting authority and founder-led delivery.
- Publish practical SAP integration, architecture, and transformation insights.
- Use training content to move readers from free education to workshops, coaching, and courses.
- Use media channels to grow reach and reinforce the creator identity without diluting consulting credibility.
- Add verified client testimonials and case studies as evidence becomes available.

## 6. SEO Strategy

Consulting: SAP consultant Canada; SAP Integration Suite consultant; SAP CPI consultant; Enterprise Architect Canada.

Training: SAP CPI training; Enterprise Architecture training; Solution Architect coaching.

Media: SAP tutorials; Architecture career coaching; Technology YouTube channel.

The shared page head includes these keyword groups, while page-specific titles and descriptions support individual search intent.

## 7. Lead Generation Strategy

High value:

- Book consultation
- Request proposal
- Schedule discovery call

Medium value:

- Download an architecture or integration guide
- Join the newsletter

Low value:

- Subscribe to YouTube
- Follow LinkedIn

## 8. Technology Stack

- Current application: Node.js, Express, and EJS.
- Hosting/domain direction: company domain with Namecheap-compatible hosting.
- Email: Microsoft 365 Business Email.
- Booking: add Calendly to the Contact flow.
- Measurement: Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console.

## 9. Revenue Alignment

| Revenue stream | Website function |
| --- | --- |
| Consulting | Lead generation and proposal requests |
| Training | Team training inquiries and workshop registration |
| Coaching | Booking system and discovery calls |
| Online courses | Course detail and registration/sales pages |
| YouTube | Audience growth and education funnel |
| Travel and media | Portfolio pages, partnerships, and sponsorship inquiries |
